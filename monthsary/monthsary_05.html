My love,

Happy 5th monthsary to us. ❤️ Five months already, and somehow every month feels like it brought us closer. Spending this one in Baguio, away from the usual noise, reminded me how much I enjoy simply being with you.

Thank you for the laughs, for your patience, and for making even the coldest, most tiring moments feel warm. The pine trees and the mountain air were beautiful, but what I'll remember most is you beside me.

I don't know every place our story will take us, but I know I want to keep walking it with you, one chapter at a time.

Happy 5th Monthsary, my love. I love you. ♡
